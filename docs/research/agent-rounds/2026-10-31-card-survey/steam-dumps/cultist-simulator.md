The entire experience takes place on a tabletop where cards appear with a block of text, and you're required to read each one, then using your imagination to piece together an actual game in your mind out of the deliberate confusion it tries hard to constantly create.

That design philosophy just doesn't work, as it adds complexity for its own sake rather than any actual depth.
