Dota Underlords - Postmortem:
Dota Underlords is Valve's version of the Dota 2 custom game Dota Auto Chess.

The origins of this "auto battler" or "auto chess" genre get a bit murky, with several mods & custom maps from Starcraft, Warcraft III, and Starcraft II cited as potential antecedents. Legion TD, Squadron TD, et al.

In January 2019, a group of Chinese developers known as Drodo Studio, released Dota Auto Chess. Drodo Studio stated they were inspired by Mahjong as well as the Warcraft III mod Pokemon Defense.

By May 2019, Dota Auto Chess had over 8 million subscribers; the hype train was full steam ahead, and Valve announced Dota Underlords.

Valve attempted to cash in on the trend by doing what they had always done: hire the mod team and make their own IP. Except this time, the devs refused. Valve's offer supposedly required Drodo to relocate to Seattle and work under an L-visa (their legal status would be dependent on working for Valve) which was a deal breaker.

Meanwhile Auto Chess was released by Drodo Studio for Android (April 2019) and iOS (May 2019) with a co-publishing partnership for mainland China shared between Drodo, Chengdu longyuan Network Technology Co. Ltd. (aka Dragonest), and Tencent (which also happens to have a minority stake in Epic Games).

Dota Underlords left Early Access on June 20, 2019, making it the first [standalone title] to market for PC.
