The tutorial explains the fundamental mechanics of the pieces in the game:

> Rooks explode in a plus shape, shooting out vertical and horizontal beams. They're a strong all-around bomb type.
> Queens explode in eight directions, controlling as much space as a rook and bishop combined. But their slow-moving projectiles can be avoided on reaction.
> Knights launch several diagonal projectiles, each an L-shaped move away from the bomb. Knights control less space than most other bombs, but their explosions stay on screen for longer.
> Kings have the shortest range of all bomb types, but they will automatically explode if anyone gets close to them. They can lock down a small area for a very long time.
> Puzzle mode can help you learn to set up chain reactions, and survival mode is a fun way to practice dodging explosions.

You can see which pieces you're placing next (like Tetris) at the top of the board. ### R21 · Recommended · 16.7 hrs on record · 4 September, 2025 · english/mostrecent
It's chess, but the pieces explode! Also, you get a cute duck to control.

I cannot recommend this game enough. It has everything one would want in a game, and the game WANTS you to succeed.
1) Good tutorial that teaches you how to play.
2) Good difficulty curve. It slowly gives you more complicated things to worry about only after you are used to the current problems. 99% of the time I failed was purely because I messed up, not because of unfair or cheating AI.
3) Fun and challenging puzzles with, again, a proper ramping of difficulty. My SO was having a blast with the puzzles, and she doesn't do puzzle games that often.
4) Lots of content. Adventure mode, gauntlet, survival, puzzles, a vs mode, even some extras after beating adventure mode!
5) Plenty of accessibility options. If you don't live and breath video games, that's fine! You can make the game easier and the game doesn't judge, because having fun is the goal of C.T. Matthews.

And that's probably the best thing I can say. I had fun the entire time I played, and the game is well worth it.

There's nothing like playing a string of poorly made games to make you appreciate the fine art and science of elegant game design. C.T. Matthews is a master. This game perfectly achieves what it set out to do, with everything you could want from the simple premise, and no additional filler.

I'm always scouring Steam and trying out new chess-themed games. 90% of them are trash. 5% are mediocre. This one is exceptional.

I love chess, but bomberman-style games are a genre I have never had the slightest interest in, even after briefly playing some classic examples. This is probably the only such game I will ever spend time with, and that's a testament to its exceptional execution and transcendence of the genre.

It seems obvious to me that so many other developers can not possibly enjoy the games they create, because the amount of talent and problem solving required to create any game far exceeds the interest even a naif could have in so many mediocre time wasters.

The first bonus note you unlock in this game makes clear why personal interest is the greatest motivator for excellence. Matthews designed this game because s/he wanted to play it, and it shows. You will too.
