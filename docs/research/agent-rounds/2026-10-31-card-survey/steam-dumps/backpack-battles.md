Game is fun in theory but the enjoyment ends when you realize that when playing ranked at higher trophy level runs you will always encounter cheat builds that were achieved using unlimited shop rolls. Example of a fake build: Ranger with Hammer build, just so happened to acquire 4 Strong Heroic potions, 4 Lucky Pigs buffing Hammer, 4 Spectral Daggers -- All being buffed by a Hero Longsword, Mega Clover with exact amount of Clovers packing bag for instant buffs, extra Mana Orbs & Snowballs to fill in the gaps, and of course the kicker to prove it's fake -- The treasure item Pumpkin (1 out of 23 treasures) which provides Stun to synergize perfectly with the build.

Will no longer be playing this game unless Devs properly address the issue.

The game is excellent. It feels genuinely unique—you can play it competitively or just relax and experiment with builds, and both approaches work. That balance is rare.

That said, I think the ranked experience could be improved, mainly around matchmaking and build consistency.

**Matchmaking (main issue)**
Right now, it feels like matchmaking doesn’t properly account for the current state of a run. In a game where players start with 5 lives, remaining lives are the clearest and most intuitive indicator of how a run is going.

If I’m at 1 life, I should mostly be facing players who are also at 1 life. Instead, it sometimes feels like I’m matched against players who had near-perfect runs and are significantly ahead in power.

I don’t think matchmaking should consider build strength, item value, or recent performance. Those systems tend to punish strong or efficient play and create the feeling that the game is “correcting” outcomes behind the scenes.

A cleaner and more competitive approach would be:

* Match primarily by MMR (rank)
* Then strongly weight remaining lives
