Stacklands is one of the most addictive indie games I’ve played in a while.

The whole game revolves around stacking cards to build a village, gather resources, and fight enemies. It sounds simple, but it quickly becomes a really satisfying mix of strategy, survival, and light automation.

The gameplay loop is super tight. You are constantly unlocking new cards, discovering recipes, and expanding your setup. It is easy to learn but has enough depth to keep you hooked for hours.

The only downside is that it can get a bit grindy later on, and managing a full board of cards can become messy. RNG from card packs can also slow you down at times.
