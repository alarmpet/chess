### R22 · Recommended · 522.2 hrs on record · 23 May · english/trendsixmonths
Been playing this for a decade. The community's still alive and thriving on discord, the game's still getting updates and mods, and it hasn't lost its interest. It's hard to build a good army of your own, but it's easy to copy other players' armies provided you have the pieces. The game balance is based on a point-buy system where you can't exceed a point total, and strong pieces cost more points to add to an army than weaker ones. There's some rock-paper-scissors interactions where one piece can't attack another, which people call nuance. Nuance forces you to build around certain piece types some to cover weaknesses, but there's enough options so that you can still set up your own piece combos to launch creative attacks for control or to deliver checkmate.
