### R22 · Recommended · 106.4 hrs on record · 21 October · english/trendmonth
A phenomenal soundtrack, which also happens to come with a good game.

No, seriously, the music in this game is absolute perfection. Every single track is incredibly catchy and memorable; even the few songs I wasn't sold on initially completely grew on me over time. Every single zone features an instrumental track that transitions into a vocal version during boss fights, with the late-game areas even having multiple tracks. I’ve added almost the entire OST to my own playlist.

As for the gameplay? It's Darkest Dungeon or X-Com, but with cats. From your home base, you breed new cats, optimizing their stats and skills before embarking on runs. Each cat can only be used once. I found that normal and even hard mode are perfectly manageable. I only lost two or three runs to bad RNG. I think the game is significantly fairer and more forgiving than Isaac.

I recommend this to any fan of tactical RPGs. And even if the genre isn't your thing, do listen to the soundtrack on YouTube.
