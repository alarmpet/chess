The problem is the map. It’s pretty bad, especially the maze-like ones. Overall, exploring the maps feels tedious and just isn’t very fun.

There are two paths:

1. Canon of Creation: Less story, misses some unique daemons, three endings that feel pretty meh.
2. Canon of Vengeance: More story, more daemons, more dialogue and choices, party members sometimes join you, and overall it feels more complete. The two endings are basically good vs. chaos, though they don’t matter that much. Also, most of the pictures shown on the Steam page are from this path.

100%ing the game means clearing all the endings and getting the unique daemon fusions, which becomes painfully tedious. But if you’re only playing once, I highly recommend Canon of Vengeance. Or just play both if you really want the full experience.

The Press Turn system is engaging and strategic as ever, and there's virtually infinite potential for build-making and team composition between the hundreds of unique demons and all the many ways you can customize them. There's also just so much love put into the presentation of every demon, from their animations to the unique moves a large portion of them have, to the Demon Haunt dialogue which as far as I can tell is almost totally unique for every single demon in the game. They even comment on ongoing story events sometimes; the amount of attention given to every single demon is insane considering there's nearly 300 of them.

Story-wise, it's probably one of the *weakest* JRPGs I've ever played. The characters are bland and forgettable, and even the "better" Vengeance story is pretty barebones compared to what you might expect from the genre. If you're looking for a compelling story and loveable characters you aren't going to find them here.
