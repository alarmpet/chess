What sets The Bazaar apart is its asynchronous design. You fight snapshots of other players’ boards, and the in-between “hours” where you shop, upgrade and plan your build run entirely on your own schedule. That makes it perfect for playing in short bursts: Got ten minutes? Squeeze in a couple of in-game hours. Interrupted? Come back later and pick up exactly where you left off. Few games respect your time this exceptionally well.

The variety of items, heroes and skills is the other big draw. Most runs are solid and satisfying, but every now and then the game hands you something unexpected and you end up assembling a complex, unlikely build that suddenly clicks and steamrolls everything in its path. Those moments are what keeps you coming back.
