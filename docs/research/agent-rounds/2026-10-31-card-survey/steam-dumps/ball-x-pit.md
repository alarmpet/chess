An excellent game with a highly addictive formula brimming with possibilities.
It blends several genres: brick-breaking, roguelite, and shoot'em up.
Each run lasts about 15–20 minutes. You break monsters in form of bricks with 2 sub-bosses and 1 main boss per level. You have to replay each level multiple times with different characters to unlock the next one.

The game is packed with options, and it was this abundance of possibilities that I really enjoyed.
There are many types of balls you can launch, they can evolve into new types, and when no further evolution is possible, you can still merge your latest creations with one another. On top of that, there are passive abilities, which can also sometimes evolve.
Between runs, you have access to a customizable base where you can unlock new characters, boost your stats (Endurance, Strength, Leadership, Speed, Dexterity, and Intelligence), and unlock various other options.
One of these options, unlocked fairly early on, lets you play with two different characters simultaneously. This allows you to combine unique traits of the 22 playable characters: such as double shots, gravity effects, fire from the back of the map, turn-based shots, shields, and more...
Unlocking a new level also unlocks new balls, passive abilities, and base upgrades.
And to avoid getting lost, an encyclopedia lists all the balls/passives and their evolutions. It was also very handy for tracking down evolutions I hadn't discovered yet.

The game's most distinct feature is its "Ball Fusion" and "Evolution" system. Unlike standard brick-breakers where you might just get a "fire" or "multi-ball" power-up, BALL x PIT allows you to mash two level-3 balls together to create entirely new ammunition.
Between runs, you engage in a base-building simulator, harvesting resources and building a diversity of structures and a "city" that in essence is the place where your character pool makes a living.

GAMEPLAY
The core loop is a forward-scrolling survival roguelite. You choose a hero and plunge into the "Pit," where you must break descending walls of enemies.
Auto-Firing & Catching: While the game auto-fires, you can manually "catch" balls to reset their cooldown instantly, incentivizing risky, aggressive positioning near the enemy front line.
