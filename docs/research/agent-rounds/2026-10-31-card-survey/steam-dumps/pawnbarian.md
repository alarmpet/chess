This game surprised me with how much fun it is. 

I love abstracts. They're my favorite type of board game to play, and I especially love chess and all of its variants. This game scratches the satisfying tactical itch you get when solving a chess puzzle, while being something else entirely. It elevates chess by adding abilities to pieces and iterative improvements to your character in a unique way that respects the source material.

The decks, the characters, the depth, and the time commitment are all perfect. I'd recommend it for fans of abstracts, and fans of rogue-likes.

Great work to the dev for a surprisingly good game at a great price.
