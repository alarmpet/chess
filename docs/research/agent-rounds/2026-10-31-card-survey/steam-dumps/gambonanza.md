Yes, but not at full price. This *really* wants to be a Balatro-like but for chess, but the ingredients don't work. 

Chess is not poker. It is not, in any way, a game of chance. Every move on the board is strategic, and even a pawn's placement can decide the outcome of a game. Poker, in contrast, is largely chance, with room for skilled play in certain areas. 

Balatro was able to make poker compelling by adding in new variables which broaden the potential for skilled play. Discards, jokers, upgrading cards and stacking the deck - all of these allow a player to manipulate the odds in their favour, allowing for strategy to occur. Once you understand the options available, that opens up a huge range of potential approaches.

In contrast, Gambonanza's introduction of random elements takes strategy *out* of chess. It does so in a number of ways - randomising pieces, adding in gambits and tiles, perma-loss for each piece on the board.
