Dicey Dungeons is a rogue-lite dungeon crawler where six contestants compete in a gameshow hosted by Lady Luck, hoping to win their greatest desire.

The primary gameplay mechanic during turn based combat encounters involves both the player and their enemy rolling dice, and then using the results to activate equipment. Some items deal damage equivalent to the dice result, others can heal or apply status effects such as burning dice (lose health when using that dice), freezing dice (highest value changes into a 1), curse (possibility for an item to fail during next turn), blind (can’t see the value of some dice), shock (need to spend a dice to unlock an item), locked dice (can’t use it on next turn), and more.

The most important thing to know, which makes this game enjoyable, is that its not simply a case of “rolling high equals win, rolling low equals lose”. Some items can be activated by any odd or any even result, some items need a maximum roll of a 3, some items take multiple dice to activate over several turns, while others allow you to re-roll dice or gain extra dice. Low value dice can be useful, and success depends more on equipping items that will let you benefit from whatever you roll. You have a maximum of 6 item slots, and each item requires either 1 or 2 slots, so you have to be strategic about what you equip. Items can be found in chests or bought from shops, and can also be upgraded to make them more powerful or smaller.
