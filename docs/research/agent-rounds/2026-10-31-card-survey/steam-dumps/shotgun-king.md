### R30 · Recommended · 13.0 hrs on record · 18 May · english/trendsixmonths
The computer is playing chess but you're not. What I mean becomes fairly obvious at the higher difficulty when you have to actually look at the board and see how the computer is going to try to trap you or how it's positioning a bishop or a queen behind a pawn. You're not playing chess; however, you are playing a turn based shooter. You are desperately trying to keep your damage and ammo replenishment up so you don't find yourself helplessly trapped in a game of chess you can't win. The game is in a tug-of-war between these two genres. You don't want to play chess. You want to keep the pieces away from you and kill the dangerous ones well before you have to think about what they're doing. The computer wants to hound you and box you in. I didn't really see the interesting dynamic until the third or fourth difficulty rank, but it's interesting when the computer starts thinking about what it's doing and really tries to get you.
