GAMEPLAY

Persona 5 Royal features a turn-based JRPG combat system built around exploiting enemy weaknesses, knocking them down, and capitalizing on those openings.

While the game offers numerous elemental affinities, most function identically outside of specific Technical attack interactions. What initially feels like a deep combat system quickly reveals itself to be shallow.

Progression is equally disappointing. Combat becomes repetitive within the first 10 hours, with little mechanical evolution to keep encounters interesting. The game even provides abilities like Insta-kill, allowing players to bypass ordinary encounters entirely. It's almost an acknowledgment of how tedious its combat eventually becomes.

STORY

The story begins with a compelling premise: a teenager wronged by society, navigating a deeply unjust world. The opening establishes intriguing characters and a promising narrative centered around rebellion and societal corruption.

Unfortunately, the writing loses momentum roughly a third of the way through. The narrative becomes predictable, character development feels repetitive, and the social commentary gradually loses its impact.

The greatest weakness is the lack of restraint. Conversations reiterate information the player already knows, story arcs drag beyond their natural conclusions, and the pacing suffers under excessive dialogue.
