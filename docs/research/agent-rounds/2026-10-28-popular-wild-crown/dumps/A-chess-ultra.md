### R3 · Recommended · 5.1 hrs on record · 78 people found this review  · 4 January, 2022
My "yes" recommendation comes with a huge set of caveat.

If all you're looking for is a casual chess game with nice graphics and decent musical score (which I was), then you won't be disappointed with this game.

If you're expecting this game to be a reincarnation of the Chessmaster series, then you will definitely be disappointed.

The chess engine on the highest setting claims to have an ELO of around 2000. It's actually more in the 1600-1800 range. It'll give you a good game if you're a casual or club player, but anything more than that and you'll bury it. It's nowhere near any of the current crop of free or commercial chess engines in strength, so don't expect to do any serious analysis
